B6 - Employees earning more than their department average

Run B6.sql with SQLite. The file creates the employees and departments tables
using the records in Dataset.csv, then runs the requested query using two CTEs:
dept_avg and above_avg.

Output:

name    dept_name      salary  avg_salary  pct_above
Farhan  Data Platform  82000   66000.0     24.24
Anita   Engineering    90000   75000.0     20.0
Chetan  Analytics      75000   64000.0     17.19

Hina is not included because she has no department ID. pct_above is the
percentage by which salary exceeds the department average.
