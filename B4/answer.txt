B4 - Salary charts

The B4.py program reads the employee records from Dataset.csv and creates
one figure arranged in a single row with two charts:

Left chart: A Seaborn histogram showing the distribution of employee salaries.
Title: Salary Distribution

Right chart: A Seaborn box plot showing salary grouped by department ID.
Title: Salary by Department

When B4.py is run, it displays the figure and saves a copy as
salary_plots.png in this folder. The chart itself is an image, so it cannot
be represented as plain text; this file describes its layout and titles.
