B3 - Highest-paid employee in each department

Pandas one-liner using idxmax:

emp.loc[emp.groupby("dept_id")["salary"].idxmax(), ["dept_id", "name", "salary"]]

Output:

 dept_id   name  salary
      10  Anita   90000
      20 Chetan   75000
      30 Farhan   82000

The runnable code is in B3.py. It reads the employee records from Dataset.csv.
Employees without a department ID are not included in this department-wise result.
